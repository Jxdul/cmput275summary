Study Method

1. Read the lecture pageUse the Big picture first, then work through the detailed sections.
2. Trace the codeOpen the listed lecture-code files and predict values, pointers, or stream state.
3. Explain pitfallsIf you cannot explain why a bug happens, reread that section before moving on.
4. Drill questionsUse known/unknown marking in Exam Practice to focus review.
